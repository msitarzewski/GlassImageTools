Where every image on this site came from. None of them shows a real person’s details.

The screenshots

Every screenshot of the app was taken in Apple’s visionOS 27.2 simulator (Apple Vision Pro), with the app driven through real taps and edits by an automated test, then cropped for this site. The unchanged editor pictures retain build-36 provenance; the opening screen is refreshed from build 37 for image-link Paste. Store images keep the full 3840 × 2160 native frame. Nothing in them was painted in afterwards.

  • The living room around the window is the simulator’s built-in environment, and the system parts of the interface (menus, sheets, the share sheet) are visionOS. Both are Apple’s.
  • The “Maple Street Community Garden” page being edited was made up for this site. The garden, the name Sam Rivera and the address at example.com, a domain reserved for examples, are all fictional.
  • The code in the second image is an illustrative Swift crop example written for this project and drawn into an image. It is demonstration content, not another app’s private document.
  • The “after” image on the home page is part of the actual PNG the app put on the clipboard during that test.
  • The descriptions under the images were written by Apple’s on-device model, running in the simulator. On a headset the same kind of model writes them, so the words may differ.

The icon

The icon, a sun and hills on red glass in a silver frame, is original artwork for this project, drawn as three SVG layers in Tools/Icon. Its glass, red and silver follow the author’s other apps, such as AudioPaper and AutoPaper.
